## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Split verdict: the marketing layer reads AI-generated within seconds; the task layer does not.**

LLM assessment (A): 7 `picsum.photos` placeholders shipped on the production home page (3 posing as meal photos with fabricated alt text, rendered `mix-blend-luminosity` — grayscale food on an appetite surface; 4 as near-invisible washes at 3–10% opacity); an icon font (`material-symbols-outlined`) that is never loaded, so "auto_awesome" renders as literal text; fabricated personalization ("your 3PM glucose dip") with a handler-less button in a Server Component; hardcoded "4.9/5" stars and macro ledger; 223 uppercase-tracked micro-kickers; an unverified Unsplash "Glazed Salmon & Quinoa Bowl" as the one interactive dish card shown to Indian lunch buyers; duplicate section numbering (`Section03*` ×2, `Section04*` ×3, `Section05*` ×2) and `data-ui-generation="stitch-74"` in production DOM.

Deterministic scan (B): 15 findings, but 12 live in `quarantine/` (verified excluded from tsconfig and never imported) and the 2 `broken-image` hits fire on code comments — **net 1 real finding in live code** (`animate-bounce`, `components/wellness/HydrationCircadianTracker.tsx:143`). The detector's CSS-level rules agree the live tree is clean of surface slop; every serious tell A found (placeholder URLs, unloaded fonts, fabricated data) is semantic and below the detector's horizon. No overlay evidence: no dev server, deps not installed.

Where they agree: the codebase's token discipline is real (near-zero raw-hex/palette findings in live code). Where the detector missed: picsum/Unsplash placeholders are valid URLs, so no rule fired — the biggest visual-integrity problem in the app is invisible to the scanner.

## Density evidence (B, deterministic)

- 316 `.tsx` files under `app/` + `components/` (300 excluding quarantine); **5 render images** via `next/image`/`<img>` (plus 2 wrapper definitions; ~31 consumers of the SafeImage wrapper, nearly all dish thumbnails).
- Home page composes ~19 landing sections; `components/landing/` (30 files) sums to **1,255 words** of static copy; with hero content ≈1,300+ words vs a typical DTC-food landing's 350–600.
- Type sizes repo-wide: `text-xs` ×609, `text-sm` ×580, `text-2xs` ×92, `text-3xs` ×136 vs `text-base` ×89 — **78% of type-size usage ≤14px; 837 instances ≤12px**, against the token file's own warning (`lib/tokens/src/tokens.css:82-86`).
- Owned photography: exactly 2 files in `public/brand/`.
- `Header.tsx` imports the 5-destination `PRIMARY_NAV` and renders 2 links; mobile account tab opens a 13-link sheet.

## Overall Impression

The owner's instinct is measurably right and has a precise shape: **this is a storefront whose task layer was built by a disciplined product team and whose marketing layer was generated by a pitch deck.** The home page literally self-describes as "3-Pillar Revenue Architecture" (code comment) — it is organized by the revenue model, not by the visitor's hunger. Text substitutes for imagery (1,300 words, 2 real photos), micro-type substitutes for hierarchy (78% of type ≤14px), and clinical vocabulary substitutes for appetite. Meanwhile the funnel *improves* with depth — trial and checkout are genuinely excellent — which is exactly backwards for acquisition: the peak (real dish rail) lands at 10% scroll and the page ends on an 84-word legal disclaimer.

## What's Working

1. **The money path is honest and calm** — one decision per screen, server-owned amounts, waitlists instead of dead ends, "Pay ₹X with UPI" as a single gold action with a real trust row.
2. **Clinical trust is structural, not decorative** — allergens never falsely affirmed, estimated macros carry "~", zero-CLS image frames on the menu.
3. **Chrome engineering** — route-group shells, back-gesture contracts, scroll-hysteresis bottom nav, AA-verified token pairs.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Fabricated imagery and content on the trust-critical home page.** 7 picsum placeholders, unverified Unsplash hero dish, fake AI recommendation with a dead button, hardcoded stars/macros, literal "auto_awesome" text. A page claiming ISO 22000 / FSSAI / ±2g precision cannot carry fabricated visuals; every real claim reads as fake. Fix: route the existing real dish-image pipeline (already used on menu/PDP/trial) into the landing layer; delete Smart Match until personalization exists; wire or remove the need-state chips; use the icon system that's actually loaded.
- **[P1] The home page is three business pitches, not a storefront.** 16 sections, ~1,300 words, ~12 competing destinations (D2C + B2B + telehealth + marketplace + recipes + community). Cut to the hunger path: hero → today's dishes → how it works → one plan module → one proof strip → FAQ (≤6 sections, ≤450 words); B2B and telehealth already have dedicated routes.
- **[P1] Plan identity fractures across the funnel.** Home sells "PCOS Hormone Balance"; the click lands on "Steady"; other rails invent third and fourth naming universes. Scent-of-information breaks exactly on the money path. Fix: one canonical display object per plan in `lib/plans.ts`, rendered everywhere.
- **[P1] Register mismatch: clinical-whitepaper voice on appetite surfaces.** "Biological footprint", "Ketogenic Depth", "Insulin Sensitivity" at the first decision point; the trial page and FAQ prove the plain-food voice exists in-house. Keep clinical vocabulary one layer down where it earns trust.
- **[P2] Micro-type density is the "dense" feeling, measured.** 837 instances ≤12px including body copy, against the token file's own rule; 223 uppercase kickers. Promote card body to `text-sm`/`text-base`; kickers ≤1 per page.
- **[P2] Desktop primary navigation is missing.** `PRIMARY_NAV` imported, never rendered; footer is the only desktop IA surface and it's hidden on mobile.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted mobile):** highest-intent chips ("Fat loss") are dead buttons; four 1920×1080 picsum downloads for 3–10%-opacity washes on cell data; Account tab opens a sheet instead of navigating. (Good: thumb-zone tabs, 44px targets, sticky pay bar.)

**Jordan (first-timer):** unexplained jargon at the first decision point; picks "PCOS Hormone Balance", lands on "Steady", assumes the click went wrong; 8 undifferentiated plan doors; literal "auto_awesome" text reads as a broken site; no visible help/contact in primary chrome.

**Riley (stress tester):** dead controls that appear functional; claims that collapse under one probe (4.9/5 with no source, alt="Weight Loss Meal" over a random photo, "Dr. Anjali Nair, RD" in a fabricated dashboard *and* as a promised consultation); refresh mid-assessment loses all 5 steps; same dish gets three different disclosure treatments.

## Minor Observations

- Satoshi loads via render-blocking Fontshare `@import` while JetBrains Mono correctly uses `next/font`.
- GoalRouter's "Just browsing" is a raw `<a>` — full page reload on a high-traffic path.
- FAQ's 84-word GLP-1 disclaimer belongs on `/legal/disclaimer`.
- Landing section files numbered out of render order with duplicate numbers — maintenance trap.
- `data-ui-generation` / `data-screen-id` stamped into production DOM.
- Footer `max-md:hidden`: FSSAI licence line unreachable on mobile except via the account sheet.
- `FlipCard` silently substitutes a different plan's per-meal price as a fallback.
- One live `animate-bounce` (HydrationCircadianTracker.tsx:143) — the only detector finding in live code.

## Questions to Consider

1. Why isn't `/trial` the model for the home page? It converts the same offer with ~10% of the words and one decision.
2. The real food photography already exists behind the image proxy — the menu proves it. Why does the landing layer fabricate imagery instead of pulling actual dishes? One `fetchMenu()` call is already made on this very page.
3. Who is the home page for — the customer or the pitch deck? What would it look like if section order followed appetite instead of the org chart?
