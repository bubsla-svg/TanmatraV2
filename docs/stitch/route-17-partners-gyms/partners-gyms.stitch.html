Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum member count required? expand_more
We require a minimum of 50 active recurring members to establish a branded tier partnership. For independent trainers, we offer a specialized affiliate structure with no minimums.
How does logistics and delivery work? expand_more
We handle end-to-end cold-chain logistics. Meals can be delivered directly to your facility's dedicated commercial fridge (provided by us) or directly to your members' homes, depending on your preferred operational model.
Is there an upfront cost? expand_more
Zero upfront cost for standard integration. Premium integrations requiring on-site kiosks or heavy API customization may incur nominal setup fees, which are offset against first-quarter commissions.
