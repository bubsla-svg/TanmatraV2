Managing glucose spikes with PCOS

I notice my levels are much higher in the morning even with a low-carb dinner. Why?

verified ANSWERED BY RD MEERA SHARMA

This is known as the Dawn Phenomenon. Even with a low-carb dinner, your liver naturally releases stored glucose in the early hours to prepare your body for the day. For those with PCOS, insulin resistance can amplify this effect. A short morning walk or a high-protein breakfast can help stabilize these levels.

Metabolic Health James L.

Walking after meals

How soon after eating should I start walking to see the best impact on my post-meal glucose?

verified ANSWERED BY RD MEERA SHARMA

Aim to start your walk within 30-45 minutes of finishing your meal. This is typically when glucose levels begin to peak. Even a gentle 10-15 minute walk can significantly blunt the glucose response by engaging your muscles to uptake circulating glucose independently of insulin.
