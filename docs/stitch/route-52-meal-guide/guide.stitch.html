STORAGE

Reheating & shelf life

Cold storage

Keep refrigerated at 4°C. Consume within 3 days of delivery.

Container safety

Lid is not microwave safe. Transfer to ceramic if heating in oven.

Microwave reheating

  1. 1

    Remove lid and sprinkle a teaspoon of water over the grains.

  2. 2

    Heat on high for 2 minutes or until steaming throughout.

  3. 3

    Let stand for 1 minute before serving to ensure even heat.
