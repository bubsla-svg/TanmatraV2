Clinical Nutritionist, Metabolic Health

"Food is the most powerful clinical intervention we have."

Dr. Ananya Nair brings nearly a decade of focused clinical experience to metabolic health, specializing in the complex interplay between diet, insulin resistance, and cellular energy systems. Her approach departs from traditional, restrictive dieting frameworks, favoring a robust, data-driven methodology that treats food as precise biochemical input.

Prior to joining the platform, Dr. Nair led the clinical nutrition department at a premier metabolic research institute, where she developed protocols for reversing pre-diabetes and managing complex PCOS phenotypes. She believes that sustainable metabolic correction requires not just nutrient restriction, but strategic nutrient partitioning.

Her clinical philosophy is rooted in the belief that metabolic health is deeply individual. She utilizes continuous glucose monitoring data alongside comprehensive lipid and hormone panels to craft highly targeted, sustainable interventions that empower patients to reclaim control over their metabolic destiny without sacrificing the joy of eating.
