Warm turmeric lentil bowl

A deeply nourishing, golden-hued bowl designed to support digestion and reduce inflammation. The earthy turmeric pairs beautifully with creamy lentils and a bright squeeze of lemon.

By Meera Rao · 24 July 2026

Time 25 min
Calories 480
Protein 32g

This bowl is a masterclass in functional comfort food. Red lentils break down quickly to create a luscious, porridge-like consistency, acting as the perfect canvas for the potent anti-inflammatory properties of turmeric and ginger. The addition of a dollop of yogurt not only provides a cooling contrast but also introduces beneficial probiotics.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • Greek yogurt or plant-based alternative

Method

  1. 1

    In a medium pot, combine the rinsed red lentils, grated ginger, turmeric, and vegetable broth.

  2. 2

    Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover, and let simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and have absorbed most of the liquid.

  3. 3

    Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the fresh spinach until it wilts into the warm lentils.

  4. 4

    Squeeze the juice of half a lemon over the mixture and stir well to incorporate the bright acidity.

  5. 5

    Ladle into bowls. Top each serving with a dollop of Greek yogurt and a sprinkle of fresh chopped cilantro. Serve warm.
