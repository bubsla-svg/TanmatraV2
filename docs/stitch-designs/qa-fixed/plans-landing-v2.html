Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip days or pause my plan? expand_more
Yes, our dashboard allows complete flexibility. You can pause your subscription or skip specific days up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery. The credits will remain in your account for future use.
What time are meals delivered? expand_more
Standard delivery takes place between 4:00 AM and 7:30 AM to ensure your breakfast is ready when you wake up. We use specialized cool-kits that maintain optimal temperature for up to 4 hours outside.
Are the containers recyclable? expand_more
We use 100% compostable bagasse containers and recyclable PET lids. Our outer delivery bags are reusable, and we collect them during your next delivery to reduce environmental impact.
